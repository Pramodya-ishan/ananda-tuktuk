# Tourist languages and WhatsApp calls

Launch with English, Simplified Chinese, Russian, French and German. Prioritize Chinese, Russian and French using both visitor volume and EF English proficiency data. Include German because Germany is a major visitor market. Hindi is outside the first release. Arrival country and aggregate proficiency do not identify an individual’s language.

- [SLTDA January–October 2026 figures](https://www.sltda.gov.lk/storage/common_media/MonthlyTouristArrivalsFinalOctober2026.11.11.pdf): India 499,625; China 123,740; Germany 105,305; Russia 89,741; France 83,902.
- [EF EPI 2025](https://www.ef.com/wwen/epi/): China 464, Russia 521, France 539, Germany 615. This is test-sample evidence, not a survey of visitors to Sri Lanka.

Keep existing English URLs and serve other locales at language-prefixed URLs. On an initial unprefixed visit, use browser language preferences in priority order, with English fallback. A manual choice overrides detection and persists. Explicit localized URLs remain shareable. Keep the user on the same page when switching and preserve saved ride details. Expose a visible language control in the desktop and mobile header, with each language written in its own script. Set the document language and provide alternate-language metadata.

Translate the public pages, stop descriptions, package advice, FAQ, controls, accessibility labels and planner. Keep stable stop IDs and leave user-entered details untouched. Use an English WhatsApp booking draft for Ananda, with the chosen language noted, and tell the guest what will open. Review translations for meaning and test mobile text wrapping.

Add a footer voice-call action separate from WhatsApp chat and regular phone calls. Use only an owner-provided WhatsApp voice call link; until configured, offer WhatsApp chat with instructions to tap the voice-call icon. Do not label a chat link as a direct call. [WhatsApp call links](https://faq.whatsapp.com/456694046556486/) can expire after 30 days of inactivity.

Verify first visits, preference precedence, manual switching, unsupported-language fallback, deep links, ride persistence, translated booking flow and footer contact options. No deployment or push is part of this change unless requested.
